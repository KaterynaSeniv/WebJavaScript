Defining Luxury

AURUM is a manifesto of modern jewelry aesthetics. Founded in Ivano-Frankivsk, our brand has become synonymous with refinement, where every line matters and every stone tells its own unique story. We don't just create jewelry — we capture moments that stay with you forever.

Craftsmanship

Each piece is handcrafted by our masters. We combine traditional casting techniques with innovative 3D modeling to achieve perfection.

Ethics

We use only certified gold and conflict-free diamonds. Your jewelry is pure in every sense, from origin to final polish.

Sustainability

We believe in responsible consumption. Our creations are designed to serve generations, transcending seasonal trends.

The Workshop

Our heart is a small studio where gold is transformed into art. We oversee every stage: from the first pencil sketch to the final buffing. This meticulous control allows us to guarantee quality without compromise.
